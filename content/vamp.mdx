vamp is an experimental, MIT-licensed visual media protocol for humans and agents. It gives tools a shared catalogue of assets, revisions, events and observations without moving or rewriting the original media.

<img src="/images/sdj-vamp-vamp-sh.png" alt="vamp.sh: A visual media protocol for humans and agents, beside a bat drawn in hexadecimal." className="max-w-full h-auto border border-gray-700 my-3 rounded" />

The name comes from jazz: a vamp is a short repeating figure that holds the ground while different players enter and leave. Here, that shared ground lets people browse and organise a library while agents collect references, keep provenance and read the owner's feedback.

<img src="/images/sdj-vamp-vampire-exploration.png" alt="An agent's exploration in Vampire: collected website references, with who added each one, its notes, source link and tags in the inspector." className="max-w-full h-auto border border-gray-700 my-3 rounded" />

Vampire is the separate Mac and iOS library app built on the protocol. The protocol is open; the application is proprietary. Both are pre-alpha.
